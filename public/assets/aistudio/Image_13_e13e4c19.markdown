## Brand & Style
This design system embodies inclusive care, dignity, and cultural familiarity for women and marginalized gender identities across India—spanning rural, semi-urban, and urban contexts. The UI bridges compassionate bedside presence with contemporary clinical intelligence. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a blend of **Tactile Warmth and Soft Modernism**. Interfaces prioritize welcoming, non-intimidating surfaces over sterile clinical whites. Visual layouts evoke safety, sisterhood (*Sakhi*), and deep respect, ensuring users navigating stigma, distress, or low digital literacy feel understood and unhurried. 

Design principles:
- **Radical Inclusivity:** Multilingual parity, assistive-screen support, screen-magnifier readiness, and explicit safety toggles (instant hide/quick exit).
- **Tactile Softness:** Pill-shaped interactions, deep-radii cards, and unhurried spacing that reduce clinical stress and health anxiety.
- **Dignified Clarity:** Medical terminology presented through clear visual hierarchy, plain language, and sympathetic iconography.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative medical grounding with comforting, restorative hues inspired by regional Indian botanical and textile traditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#5E3B4D` - Muted Plum):** The anchor of trust, authority, and safety. Used for primary navigational bars, structural headers, high-emphasis text, and active interactive borders.
- **Secondary (`#D9777F` - Dusty Rose):** Warmth and human empathy. Used for key action buttons, cycle tracking focal points, and urgent empathetic notices.
- **Tertiary (`#7A9A8B` - Sage Green):** Restoration, wellness, and affirmative outcomes. Deployed for fertility and pregnancy health tags, verification badges, prescription refills, and clinical validation.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFDF9` - Warm Ivory):** Eliminates cold hospital glare. Replaces raw white (`#FFFFFF`) as the base canvas to comfort sensitive eyes during postpartum or late-night consultations.
- **Supportive Accents (`#EDE7F6` / `#9C88B9` - Soft Lavender):** Used for non-threatening interactive containers, AI chat bubbles, mental wellness journals, and reflective spaces.
- **Neutral Dark (`#241A20` - Deep Bramble):** Ensures high-legibility AAA contrast against warm ivory without the stark harshness of pure black.

### High Contrast & Emergency Modes
The system reserves `#C93B3B` (Crimson) exclusively for clinical emergencies and abuse hotlines. For low-vision or outdoors/bright-sunlight conditions typical of field-worker tablets, a high-contrast mode switch remaps surfaces to `#FFFFFF` with text shifting directly to `#120A0F` with `#3A192B` outlines.

## Typography
Typography is tuned for readability across diverse reading proficiencies, multi-script layouts (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali), and device tiers.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Evokes maternal grace, editorial dignity, and human warmth. Used sparingly for greetings, article titles, milestone celebrations, and empathetic section anchors.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for wide counters, clear aperture distinctions, and exceptional rendering on lower-resolution Android displays. It remains completely legible even at smaller scale for prescription directions, dosage instructions, and AI advisory dialogs.
- **Script Handling:** When rendering Indic typography, optical line-heights automatically expand by 1.25x across all levels to prevent diacritic clipping.

## Layout & Spacing
A mobile-first fluid layout accounts for one-handed thumb navigation common in crowded clinics or private home environments. 

### Grid & Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (up to 599px):** 4-column layout. Minimum touch target dimension of 48px × 48px to accommodate users with motor disabilities or tremors. Bottom sheets replace modals. Sticky bottom action bars host urgent callouts (e.g., "Speak with HerSakhi AI" or "Emergency Consult").
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Adapts for rural healthcare worker (ASHA) viewports, facilitating split-screen intake forms alongside patient medical history.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1200px` to maintain focused line lengths for clinical reading.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm is deliberate and unhurried. Ample vertical breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) separates sensitive inputs, preventing cognitive overload and visual intimidation during symptom logging.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on soft ambient warmth rather than aggressive drop shadows or sterile high-tech elevation.

### Ambient Warmth Stack
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FFFDF9` surface with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** Tonal layering using `#FFFFFF` or lavender tint `#EDE7F6` with a diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(94, 59, 77, 0.06)`. This subtle plum tint creates an organic, radiant lift.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Care Floating Bars):** `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(94, 59, 77, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Emergency Drawers & Overlays):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(36, 26, 32, 0.20)` over a soft plum-tinted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(94, 59, 77, 0.35)`).

### Tactile Low-Contrast Outlines
To assist users on glare-heavy phone screens, elevated surfaces pair with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(94, 59, 77, 0.08)`. This maintains structural clarity without adding visual noise.

## Shapes
Sharp corners create visual tension; SheShines adopts an ultra-soft, organic shape language that reflects organic forms, safety, and physical comfort.

- **Base Radii:** Interactive buttons, pills, chips, and quick-action triggers use full capsules (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Cards & Enclosures:** Containers utilize deep curvature (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 24px - 32px), creating smooth, welcoming contours reminiscent of hand-crafted clay and woven cloth.
- **Form Inputs:** Fields feature generous 16px to 20px radii, rejecting sharp rectangular boxes to reduce bureaucratic form anxiety.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Care CTA):** Dusty Rose background (`#D9777F`), text in white (`#FFFFFF`), fully pill-shaped. Hover/active states tint downward to `#C3646C`. Focus state displays a 3px ring in `#5E3B4D` offset by 2px warm ivory.
- **Secondary (Consultation / AI):** Muted Plum outline (`1.5px solid #5E3B4D`), text in `#5E3B4D`, transparent background or light lavender tint (`#EDE7F6`).
- **Voice Assist Button:** Floating circular trigger (56px × 56px) featuring a microphone icon with an ambient Sage Green (`#7A9A8B`) pulsing aura, inviting illiterate or low-vision users to speak their symptoms.

### Chips & Symptom Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-md` horizontally.
- Unselected: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(94, 59, 77, 0.15)`, text `#5E3B4D`.
- Selected: Background `#5E3B4D`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by a smooth checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Health Selectors
- Background is crisp `#FFFFFF` against the `#FFFDF9` canvas.
- Min-height 52px for finger-tap accessibility. Floating micro-labels render in `#5E3B4D` at `label-md`.
- Generous internal padding (`space-md`), with integrated voice-to-text microphones and regional script toggle buttons inside the trailing input slot.

### Cards & Care Modules
- **Health Feed Card:** Pure white background, 24px rounded corners, surrounded by plum-tinted ambient shadow. Inner padding of `space-lg`.
- **AI Companion Card (*HerSakhi*):** Soft lavender gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #EDE7F6 0%, #FFFDF9 100%)`) with a thin border in `#9C88B9`. Houses conversational prompts, empathetic reassurance, and discreet symptom analysis.

### Toggles, Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes and radios boast larger touch surfaces (24px × 24px visual box with 48px hit area).
- Active state filled with Sage Green (`#7A9A8B`) for affirming choices or Muted Plum (`#5E3B4D`).

### Specialized Inclusivity Components
- **Discreet Emergency Quick-Exit:** Permanent subtle header trigger labeled "Quick Exit / Weather View" that instantaneously clears the viewport to a neutral daily dashboard.
- **Inclusive Gender Identity & Pronoun Selectors:** Fluid multi-select chips recognizing non-binary, transmasculine, and transfeminine reproductive health needs with gender-neutral clinical language options.
